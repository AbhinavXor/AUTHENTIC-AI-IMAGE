# Authentic AI — Natural Motion System V11

## Scope

Natural Motion V11 preserves the existing Authentic AI light interface and adds a reusable, truthful motion layer across the product. It does not redesign the sidebar, header, composer layout, artifact operations, or backend generation pipeline.

## Included improvements

- Natural dotted-orb animation primitives for thinking, search, visual rendering, document generation, voice launch, and success states.
- Truthful assistant status pills:
  - Thinking while no response content is available.
  - Responding once streamed content is visible.
  - Real artifact-job stages for reading, structuring, rendering, composing, and quality checking.
- Natural chart-loading feedback.
- Assistant-avatar idle and active pulse treatment.
- Composer focus breathing, working edge motion, attachment arrival, tactile controls, and send-arrow motion.
- Sherry launch ripple and compact launch feedback from both the composer and top navigation.
- Light-theme document workflow with restrained progress motion and real stage completion feedback.
- One-time result reveal and download-button shimmer when an artifact becomes ready.
- Subtle navigation, recent-item, copy, feedback, and button micro-interactions.
- Mobile tuning and `prefers-reduced-motion` accessibility support.

## Truthfulness rules

- The interface does not claim that the microphone is listening before the Sherry workspace is opened.
- Detailed document states are driven by the real artifact job stage.
- Normal chat shows only thinking or responding states supported by current streaming state.
- Animation never replaces errors, cancellation, or actual progress values.
